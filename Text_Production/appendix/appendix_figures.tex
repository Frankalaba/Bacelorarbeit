In the enteric fermentation process of ruminants \ch{CH4} are formed through methanogenic \gls{archa}. One discussed mitigation strategy to reduce mathane resulting from cattle farming is to immunisize the cattle against these specific \gls{archa} \citep{malyuginaMitigationStrategiesMethane2025}. For developing a specific immunization strategy, the isolated methanogenic archaea strains from cattle rumen has to be well understood. Although some strains have already been isolated from the rumen of cattle, they have hardly been studied to date \citep{baca-gonzalezAreVaccinesSolution2020}. This bachelor’s thesis aims to address this issue.\\
In doing so, the focus will primarily be on two strains: \acrfull{thau} and \acrfull{bovi}.
Besides these two main strains, some experiements were also done on \acrfull{woli} and \acrfull{milli}. These two methanogenic \gls{archa} were also isolated from the rumen of cattle. Goal of the investigation, that took place, was to detect several basic characteristics and try to implement first genetic engineering tools.
